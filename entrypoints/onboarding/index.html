Welcome to Anagram

Anagram answers “did a human write this?” as you browse: every analyzed paragraph gets a small chip with its estimated extent of AI editing and a matching underline — quiet on human text, louder as the share of AI writing grows.

How it reads

moreover, this underscores a broader point… 38% AI ×2
Delving into the rich tapestry of interconnected factors… 96% AI

Reading the verdicts

Human Lightly edited Heavily edited AI-generated Unavailable

The model (EditLens, ICLR 2026) rates how much of a paragraph was written or rewritten by AI. Hover (or tap) a chip for the full readout: the four-way probability split behind the verdict and the word count. Every number is an estimate, not proof. Snippets under ~50 words are only scored when grouped with neighbors — a ×2 on a chip means it covers such a group.

The floating ball

Lives bottom-right on every page. Click it to show or hide all marks instantly. Drag it anywhere — it snaps to the nearest edge and tucks itself half-away when idle; hover brings it back. Its red counter opens the triage panel: filter by verdict, jump to each flagged paragraph, Copy report, or turn Anagram off for this site — all inside the panel.

Power moves

  • Analyze selection — select text, right-click, done. Works inside text boxes and editors too.
  • Alt+Shift+P — toggle Anagram on the current page.
  • Flagged only — switch Show → Flagged only in the popup and calm pages stay untouched; marks appear only where something was flagged.

Google Docs

Docs is an editor, not a page — so Anagram brings its own reading surface. Click Analyze document on the floating ball and a reading mode opens right in the tab, with every paragraph of the document analyzed. No navigation, and your file is never touched. Press Esc or Back to editor to return instantly — or use Open as page if you prefer a separate tab.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your browser. Analysis happens inside the extension; requests carry only a hostname and language hint by design — never full URLs.

Make it yours

The popup and options page hold the rest: pick a marking style (underline or a soft tint) and choose the analysis scope (whole page or main content only).
