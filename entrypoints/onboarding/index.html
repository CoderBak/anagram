Each paragraph gets a chip with a score from .00 (human) to 1.0 (AI-generated), in its word's colour. Hover the chip for the details. AI-generated paragraphs are flagged: the toolbar menu lists them, and the number on its icon counts them.

Tip: arXiv papers read most precisely in their HTML version, at arxiv.org/html/.

Anagram uses Pangram's open EditLens model (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Commercial use is not permitted. It should not be used to enforce any AI-usage policy in an educational or professional setting.
