Welcome to Pangram

Pangram labels the text you read with a small confidence chip — like 87% AI — right after each analyzed paragraph, answering "did a human write this?" as you browse.

Reading the verdicts

Human AI-Assisted AI Insufficient

Hover (or tap) a chip for the full readout: the estimate's credible interval, a p-value against the human baseline, and how many words were analyzed. Every number is a calibrated estimate, not proof. Text under 50 words is only analyzed when it can be grouped with its neighbors — short fragments are unreliable to judge alone.

The floating ball

Bottom-right of every page: click it to show/hide everything instantly, drag it anywhere (it remembers per site), and click its red counter to open the flagged-paragraphs panel — jump straight to anything marked AI. Prefer calm pages? Switch Show → Flagged only in the popup.

Power moves

Select any text → right-click → Analyze selection with Pangram — works even in editors and comment boxes.
Press Alt+Shift+P to toggle the overlay.
On Google Docs, the ball offers a reading view where every paragraph of your document gets analyzed.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your browser. Analysis happens inside the extension; requests carry only a hostname and language hint by design — never full URLs.
