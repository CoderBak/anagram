Welcome to Anagram

Anagram answers “how much of this did an AI write?” as you browse: every analyzed paragraph gets a small chip with a number — the model's estimate of the extent of AI editing — and the page itself is left as it was, except for a thin line under the text that was flagged.

Guide

Reading the verdicts

The model (EditLens, ICLR 2026) rates how far a paragraph sits between untouched human writing (.00) and fully AI-generated text (1.0). The number is not a share of words written by AI — it is the model's estimate of how much AI editing changed the text as a whole. Hover (or tap) a chip for the full readout: the four-way probability split behind the verdict and the word count. EditLens reads English only: other languages get a gray chip with the detected language code instead of a number. Every number is an estimate, not proof. Snippets under ~50 words are only scored when grouped with neighbors, and a short post is scored as a whole — a ×2 on a chip means it covers that many paragraphs.

The floating ball

Lives bottom-right on every page Anagram runs on. Click it to show or hide all marks instantly. Drag it anywhere — it snaps to the nearest edge and tucks itself half-away when idle; hover brings it back. Its red counter opens the triage panel: filter by verdict, jump to each flagged paragraph, Copy report, or turn Anagram off for this site — all inside the panel.

Power moves
  • Analyze selection — select text, right-click, done. Works inside text boxes and editors too.
  • Alt+Shift+P — toggle Anagram on the current page.
  • Alt+Shift+L — open the flagged-paragraphs list, keyboard already inside it. Alt+Shift+J and Alt+Shift+K walk to the next and previous flagged paragraph without touching the mouse.
  • Flagged only — switch Show → Flagged only in the popup and calm pages stay untouched; marks appear only where something was flagged.
Google Docs

Docs is an editor, not a page — so Anagram brings its own reading surface. Click Analyze document on the floating ball and a reading mode opens right in the tab, with every paragraph of the document analyzed. No navigation, and your file is never touched. Press Esc or Back to editor to return instantly — or use Open as page if you prefer a separate tab.

PDFs

A PDF keeps its own pages: Anagram draws them exactly as they are — figures, formulas, columns — and lays the chips and marks over them. Click Analyze PDF on the floating ball, Read this PDF in the popup, or right-click a link to one and choose Open PDF with Anagram. Short paragraphs are read together as on any page, a password-protected file asks for its password, and the file stays on your computer. For a PDF that is already on your computer, open the reading mode from the options page and drop the file in.

Privacy

Scoring runs in the Local engine on your computer. Model downloads are separate from analysis; page text is not sent to an online service. Website access is optional, and analysis requests use a hostname and language hint, never a full URL.

Make it yours

The popup and options page hold the rest: pick a marking style (flagged text only or every analyzed paragraph), choose the analysis scope (whole page or main content only), and check the local engine's status.
