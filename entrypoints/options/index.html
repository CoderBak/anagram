About & shortcuts

Alt+Shift+P Show or hide the Anagram overlay on the current page. Alt+Shift+L Open the flagged-paragraphs list and put the keyboard in it. Alt+Shift+J Jump to the next flagged paragraph. Alt+Shift+K Jump to the previous flagged paragraph. Right-click Choose “Analyze selection with Anagram” to score just the highlighted text. Right-click Choose “Analyze this page with Anagram” to analyze a switched-off page once.

Rebind any of these in your browser's extension shortcuts (Chrome: chrome://extensions/shortcuts).

Scoring runs in the Local engine on this computer. The browser connects to it directly; page text is not sent to an online service.

Network access and how to verify it

Text analysis runs on this computer. Anagram has no analytics or cloud scoring service.

Installation, model downloads and requested component updates use the internet. Your browser may also check for extension updates.

Opening an online PDF or Google Doc may fetch the original document again. A document’s images or links may also use the network. This is separate from uploading text for analysis.

  1. Finish local-engine setup and the model download.
  2. Disconnect from the internet, then open the reader and choose a PDF from this computer.
  3. The PDF and analysis should still work. This verifies offline operation; it is not an audit of all possible network activity.
