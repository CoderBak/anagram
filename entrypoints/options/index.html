Detection

What gets analyzed, and how paragraphs are grouped.

Per-site rules below override this default. Toggling applies to new page loads and open tabs.

PDF tabs open in the reading mode.

Main content only analyzes the article region — found the way Firefox Reader View does (Mozilla Readability) — and skips comments/sidebars outside it. Falls back to whole page when no clear region exists.

A short post or comment is analyzed as a whole, and paragraphs under the 50-word evidence floor together with their neighbors in the same article — the chip shows ×N; never across posts, comments or quotations. Off: every paragraph by itself, short ones skipped entirely.

How verdicts are drawn on the page.

Colors each paragraph by verdict — green = human, yellow = lightly edited, orange = heavily edited, red = AI-generated. Badges stay either way.

How analyzed paragraphs are marked in place. “Mark analyzed paragraphs” above stays the master on/off.

Every paragraph is analyzed either way. “Flagged only” keeps pages calm and marks just heavily-edited and AI-generated verdicts.

Scoring daemon

Verdicts come from the local anagramd daemon (EditLens roberta-large, running on this computer). Start it with ~/.anagram/bin/anagram start (or npm run serve from a source checkout). When it is not running, paragraphs show as Unavailable and are retried automatically. There is no other backend.

Where anagramd listens. Local addresses only — page text never leaves this computer. Default http://127.0.0.1:8765.

About & shortcuts

Alt+Shift+P Show or hide the Anagram overlay on the current page. Alt+Shift+L Open the flagged-paragraphs list and put the keyboard in it. Alt+Shift+J Jump to the next flagged paragraph. Alt+Shift+K Jump to the previous flagged paragraph. Right-click Choose “Analyze selection with Anagram” to score just the highlighted text. Right-click Choose “Analyze this page with Anagram” to analyze a switched-off page once.

Rebind any of these in your browser's extension shortcuts (Chrome: chrome://extensions/shortcuts).

Nothing leaves your computer — scoring runs in the local anagramd daemon on this computer. The daemon URL is restricted to local addresses.
